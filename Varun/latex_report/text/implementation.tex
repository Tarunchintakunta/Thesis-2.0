\chapter{Implementation}

The implementation lives under \texttt{Varun/s3-predictive-optimization/} and is driven by
\texttt{make pilot|baseline|improved} with \texttt{DRY\_RUN=1} by default.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{src/runner/experiment.py} --- orchestrates workload, recommend, forecast
          (holdout), savings, and evaluation; writes
          \texttt{results/data/*\_results.json}.
    \item \texttt{src/forecasting/} --- Prophet wrapper and Beck naive persistence baseline.
    \item \texttt{src/recommendation/} --- rules + XGBoost.
    \item \texttt{src/savings/} + \texttt{src/pricing/} --- list-price estimator (not live
          Pricing API).
    \item \texttt{tests/} --- pytest suite including holdout hygiene tests.
    \item \texttt{terraform/} --- scaffold only; \textbf{not applied}.
\end{itemize}

No \texttt{src/metadata/} collector and no \texttt{analysis/statistics.py} Wilcoxon module
are committed; do not claim them. Live AWS verification remains gated.
