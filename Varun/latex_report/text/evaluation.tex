\chapter{Evaluation}

\section{Data provenance}

Committed metrics come from three evidence layers:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Local simulator} (\texttt{DRY\_RUN=1}):
          \texttt{s3-predictive-optimization/results/data/\{pilot,baseline,improved\}\_results.json}.
    \item \textbf{Live lite probe} (2026-09-20, \texttt{eu-west-1}):
          \texttt{s3-predictive-optimization/results/live/live\_lite\_summary.json}
          (paired raw vectors in \texttt{live\_lite\_raw.json}).
    \item \textbf{Full live evaluations e1--e3:}
          \texttt{results/live/evaluation\_r\{1,2,3\}/summary.json} with aggregate
          \texttt{BASELINE\_COMPARE.md} /
          \texttt{baseline\_compare\_e1\_e2\_e3.json}.
\end{enumerate}

Forecast evaluation uses a \textbf{temporal holdout}
(\texttt{eval\_protocol=temporal\_holdout}): Prophet and naive persistence are fit on the
prefix of the synthetic daily-cost series and scored on the held-out suffix.
An earlier protocol compared both models to a synthetic growing series while costs
were rounded to four decimals (flattening sub-cent series); that protocol is withdrawn.

\section{Full live evaluations e1--e3}

Protocol \texttt{ca2\_three\_workload\_wilcoxon}
($n{=}10$ trials $\times$ 80 objects per workload; mode \texttt{live\_aws}):
compare our recommender cost to \textbf{AWS Lifecycle Policies} and
\textbf{Intelligent-Tiering} on
\texttt{static\_archival}, \texttt{mixed\_access}, and \texttt{high\_churn}.
Each evaluation folder reports \texttt{meets\_ca2\_two\_of\_three=true}.
Aggregate means and gates are locked to
\texttt{baseline\_compare\_e1\_e2\_e3.json} /
\texttt{BASELINE\_COMPARE.md}.

\textbf{Rebuild disclosure.} Artefact notes state summaries were rebuilt from printed
trial costs after a mid-run results-dir wipe; e1--e3 store the \emph{same} rebuilt
per-trial cost vectors. Claims below use that single rebuilt matrix across the three
evaluation artefacts --- not three independently resampled mean vectors.

\section{RQ and objectives assessment (evidence-bound)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Supported:} local predictive pipeline (recommend / forecast / savings)
          with temporal-holdout \texttt{beats\_naive=true} on all three dry-run arms.
    \item \textbf{Supported (scoped):} live cost cut versus \emph{both} native baselines on
          two of three workloads across e1--e3 artefacts
          (\texttt{meets\_ca2\_two\_of\_three=true} for evaluations 1, 2, and 3).
    \item \textbf{Not supported:} universal improvement under high churn versus Lifecycle
          (little/no improvement; non-significant Wilcoxon).
    \item \textbf{Not claimed:} Cost Explorer--settled production bill savings from the
          trial matrix (lite CE window remains an account probe, not campaign savings).
\end{itemize}

\section{What is and is not evidenced}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Evidenced (dry-run):} recommendation + forecast + savings pipeline on
          synthetic workloads; naive persistence baseline (Beck et al.); local list-price
          savings; holdout \texttt{beats\_naive=true} on pilot, baseline, and improved.
    \item \textbf{Evidenced (live lite):} real S3 PUT/GET to STANDARD vs STANDARD\_IA;
          CE/CW API reachability; paired Wilcoxon on modeled per-object storage cost and
          live latency ($n{=}24$).
    \item \textbf{Evidenced (full e1--e3):} three-workload Wilcoxon vs Lifecycle and
          Intelligent-Tiering; two-of-three success; honest high-churn null vs Lifecycle.
    \item \textbf{Not evidenced / limited:} independently resampled means per evaluation
          folder (rebuild-identical vectors); CE-settled campaign USD; long-lived Inventory
          job as the measurement path.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations and implications}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Rebuild identity:} e1--e3 share one rebuilt trial-cost matrix; multi-eval
          folders document protocol completion and gate checks, not independent sampling
          variance across evaluations.
    \item \textbf{Workload boundary:} high churn is a real failure mode versus Lifecycle;
          practitioners should not assume predictive tiering beats native policies under
          rapid object turnover.
    \item \textbf{Cost semantics:} trial and list-price deltas are not interchangeable with
          settled CE Unblended bills.
    \item \textbf{ML allocation:} improved-arm allocation accuracy $0.178$ remains weak even
          where cost Wilcoxon succeeds --- recommendation quality and cost-cut significance
          are not the same claim.
\end{enumerate}

Simulated, modeled, and trial storage deltas must not be read as production Cost Explorer
bills. Negative/null high-churn results are retained deliberately (handbook: include
negative results).
