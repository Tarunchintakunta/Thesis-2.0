\chapter{Evaluation}

\section{Data provenance}

Committed metrics come from three evidence layers:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Local simulator} (\texttt{DRY\_RUN=1}):
          \texttt{s3-predictive-optimization/results/data/\{pilot,baseline,improved\}\_results.json}.
    \item \textbf{Live lite probe} (2026-09-20, \texttt{eu-west-1}):
          \texttt{s3-predictive-optimization/results/live/live\_lite\_summary.json}
          (paired raw vectors in \texttt{live\_lite\_raw.json}).
    \item \textbf{Local three-workload Wilcoxon} (CA2 §3.3 protocol, simulator trial-buckets):
          \texttt{results/data/multi\_workload\_wilcoxon.json}.
\end{enumerate}

The live round is a \textbf{lite scope} probe (24 objects $\times$ 2 storage classes).
No live S3 Inventory \emph{job} was run. Metadata modules on disk parse lite JSON,
Inventory CSV, and Boto3 listings (moto-tested); they do not invent CE-settled savings.

Forecast evaluation uses a \textbf{temporal holdout}
(\texttt{eval\_protocol=temporal\_holdout}): Prophet and naive persistence are fit on the
prefix of the synthetic daily-cost series and scored on the held-out suffix.
An earlier protocol compared both models to a synthetic growing series while costs
were rounded to four decimals (flattening sub-cent series); that protocol is withdrawn.

\section{Local three-workload Wilcoxon (simulator)}

CA2 names static/archival, mixed-access, and high-churn trial buckets ($n{\approx}10$).
\texttt{analysis/statistics.py} ran $n{=}10$ synthetic buckets $\times$ 80 objects against
Lifecycle and Intelligent-Tiering (\texttt{multi\_workload\_wilcoxon.json}).
Significant cost cut vs \emph{both} natives on \textbf{static\_archival} and \textbf{mixed\_access}
($p{=}0.00195$); high-churn vs Lifecycle is \textbf{not} significant ($p{=}0.557$).
Simulator success bar ``$\ge 2$ of 3 workloads vs both natives'' is met.
Mean recommend+compare overhead $\approx 0.2$\,ms. These cells are \textbf{not} live AWS bills.

\section{What is and is not evidenced}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Evidenced (dry-run):} recommendation + forecast + savings pipeline on
          synthetic workloads; naive persistence baseline (Beck et al.); local list-price
          savings; holdout \texttt{beats\_naive=true} on pilot, baseline, and improved.
    \item \textbf{Evidenced (live lite):} real S3 PUT/GET to STANDARD vs STANDARD\_IA;
          CE/CW API reachability; paired Wilcoxon on modeled per-object storage cost and
          live latency ($n{=}24$); lite metadata/statistics modules over archived JSON.
    \item \textbf{Evidenced (local 3-workload):} Wilcoxon protocol vs Lifecycle and
          Intelligent-Tiering on three CA2 mixes; operational overhead timed.
    \item \textbf{Not evidenced (live):} S3 Inventory \emph{job} CSV from AWS; CE-settled
          campaign savings; live trial-buckets for the three workloads
          (see \texttt{DESIGN\_RATIONALE\_BEYOND\_CA2.md}).
\end{itemize}
