\chapter{Conclusion}
This work delivers a reproducible S3 FinOps artefact: rule and ML recommenders, Prophet versus naive forecasting (temporal holdout), a list-price savings estimator, a live lite AWS probe, and three full live evaluation artefacts (\texttt{evaluation\_r1}--\texttt{r3}) under the CA2 three-workload Wilcoxon protocol against Lifecycle Policies and Intelligent-Tiering.

\textbf{Evidence locked to JSON/MD.} Dry-run pilot / baseline / improved allocation accuracies $0.320$ / $0.504$ / $0.178$; holdout MAPE $0.016$ / $0.006$ / $0.231$ versus naive $0.44$; \texttt{beats\_naive=true} on all three arms. Simulated savings percentages ($42\%$, $21\%$, $57\%$) must not be read as Cost Explorer or production bills.

\textbf{Live lite (measured).} A destroy-after probe (\texttt{results/live/live\_lite\_summary.json}) recorded STANDARD vs STANDARD\_IA PUT/GET means, CE Unblended sum $\$3.6\times10^{-9}$ over a lagged account window, CloudWatch Logs success, and Wilcoxon $p{<}10^{-6}$ at $n{=}24$ on modeled cost and live latency.

\textbf{Full evaluations e1--e3 (measured artefacts).}
 Aggregate baseline compare
(\texttt{BASELINE\_COMPARE.md}, \texttt{baseline\_compare\_e1\_e2\_e3.json}) shows
\texttt{static\_archival} and \texttt{mixed\_access} beat both natives
(mean $\Delta$ vs Lifecycle $0.005119$ / $0.003180$; vs IT $0.000794$ / $0.004843$;
significant on all three evaluation artefacts). \texttt{high\_churn} shows
\textbf{little/no improvement} versus Lifecycle (mean $\Delta{\approx}0.000065$;
Wilcoxon $p{=}0.557$, not significant) while remaining significant versus Intelligent-Tiering
(mean $\Delta{\approx}0.0158$). Each evaluation reports
\texttt{meets\_ca2\_two\_of\_three=true}. Summaries disclose rebuild-from-log after a
results-dir wipe; e1--e3 share the rebuilt trial-cost vectors.

\section{RQ and objectives (evidence-bound)}

The predictive storage-class and cost-forecast objectives are \textbf{supported} on the
local simulator and on two of three live workloads versus both AWS-native baselines.
The objective of consistent live cost reduction under \emph{all} tested access patterns is
\textbf{not} met: high churn versus Lifecycle is a disclosed null. Settled CE production
savings are \textbf{not} claimed from the trial matrix.

\section{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Rebuild-identical e1--e3 cost vectors limit claims of independent multi-eval
          sampling variance.
    \item high\_churn versus Lifecycle is non-significant (little/no improvement).
    \item Trial / list-price costs $\neq$ CE-settled bills; lite CE window $\neq$ campaign savings.
    \item Improved ML allocation accuracy ($0.178$) remains a separate weakness from Wilcoxon
          cost-cut successes on archival and mixed workloads.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Handoff.} \texttt{GENAI\_HANDOFF.md} is deliberately \textbf{not} written here;
three evaluation artefacts exist on main, but handoff remains deferred until policy says OK.
No new AWS campaigns are implied by this conclusion.
