\chapter*{Abstract}

The highest published microservice root cause analysis (RCA) detectors rely on deep learning models trained on large labeled telemetry corpora. A newly deployed, cost-constrained serverless service has no such corpus and no training-compute budget, rendering these advanced methods inapplicable—not merely expensive. This research evaluates the accuracy-overhead position of an untrained, lightweight hybrid fault detection and localisation method for AWS serverless microservices relative to learned deep baselines.

We propose a hybrid approach combining rule-based detection (CloudWatch 3-sigma thresholds on Lambda metrics) with unsupervised machine learning localisation (Isolation Forest trained dynamically on control-period metrics). We establish a three-leg evaluation protocol: (1) comparison against Xing et al.'s (2025) published deep learning ceiling (F1 = 93.8\%, citation arm only), (2) like-for-like evaluation on the RCAEval benchmark against BARO, CIRCA, and TraceRCA ($n{=}90$) with CausalRCA incomplete ($n{=}4$ committed), and (3) monitoring overhead measurement --- Leg~3 live AWS \textbf{lite} executed (\texttt{results/live/overhead.json}; policy vs full volume reduction $=0.803$).

Our findings (evidence-locked): rule-arm F1 $=0.469$ and AC@3 $=0.611$ on RCAEval ($n{=}90$) vs BARO/CIRCA AC@3 $=0.878$ --- \textbf{not} within 10\,pp of the deep ceiling. Lite Leg~3 live overhead shows policy vs full volume reduction $=0.803$ ($\geq$50\% bar met on the lite cell). The joint CA2 competitiveness rule therefore still fails on accuracy. The hybrid localiser underperforms rules on Top-3. CausalRCA remains quarantined ($n{=}4$).

This work contributes: (1) a novel lightweight hybrid detection-localisation method avoiding deep learning's training requirements and causal inference's computational complexity, (2) a fair comparison protocol bridging trained and untrained methods across accuracy and overhead dimensions, and (3) empirical validation that competitive fault detection is achievable for newly deployed serverless services under tight cost constraints.

\textbf{Keywords:} Microservices, Serverless Computing, Fault Detection, Root Cause Analysis, AWS Lambda, CloudWatch, X-Ray, Lightweight Machine Learning
