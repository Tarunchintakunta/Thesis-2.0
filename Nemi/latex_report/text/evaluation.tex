This section reports \textbf{committed experimental results only}. Docker/Kubernetes were not executed.

\subsection{Real UNSW-NB15 training-partition sample}

A public mirror of the official UNSW-NB15 \emph{training-set} CSV was downloaded (\texttt{results/unsw\_real/DATA\_PROVENANCE.json}: \texttt{kind=real}, $\approx 175{,}341$ rows, $45$ columns). A stratified sample of $25{,}000$ rows (39 numeric features after dropping non-numeric columns) was evaluated for 30 rounds/epochs with 5 federated clients. This is \textbf{not} the full $2.5$M-flow corpus.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Centralised} & \textbf{Baseline FL} & \textbf{SecureFL-IDS} \\
\midrule
Accuracy & 0.9452 & 0.8934 & 0.6800 \\
F1-Score & 0.9603 & 0.9262 & 0.8095 \\
Avg Comm (MB/round) & 0.0 (N/A) & 3.08 & 3.12 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Real UNSW training-partition sample (\texttt{results/unsw\_real/results.json})}
\label{tab:unsw_real_results}
\end{table}

\textbf{Honest interpretation (real sample):}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Centralised CNN leads detection metrics on this sample (accuracy $0.9452$, F1 $0.9603$).
    \item Baseline FL remains competitive (accuracy $0.8934$, F1 $0.9262$) while avoiding raw data centralisation.
    \item Improved SecureFL-IDS \emph{plateaued} at accuracy $0.6800$ / F1 $0.8095$---reported honestly; it does \textbf{not} outperform baseline FL here.
    \item Communication for improved remains slightly higher than baseline ($3.12$ vs $3.08$~MB/round).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What was not evaluated}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Full $2.5$M-flow UNSW corpus / 50-round campaigns (optional depth)
    \item Privacy--utility sweep tables with fabricated $\epsilon$ rows
    \item Docker Compose or Kubernetes deployments (beyond-CA2)
    \item Multi-instance WAN FL / 50-round cloud campaigns (beyond-CA2)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Live cloud FL is lite (3 rounds / 2 in-process clients); not a confirmatory 50-round WAN study
    \item Improved arm underperforms baseline FL on the 30-round real training-partition sample
    \item Binary classification focus in committed runs
    \item No production AWS claim; stack destroyed after the lite round
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Threats to validity}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Single configuration per campaign; not multi-seed averaged
    \item Stratified $25$k sample may not match full-corpus behaviour
    \item Lite cloud round times are seconds-scale and do not estimate production training cost
\end{itemize}

The controlled experiments validate that centralised and federated arms run on synthetic and real training-partition data, and that a destroy-after-round AWS lite FL path emits S3/CloudWatch artefacts. They do \textbf{not} establish Kubernetes production readiness.
