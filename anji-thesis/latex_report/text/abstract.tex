\chapter*{Abstract}
This project introduces a novel approach to 'Reliability and Recovery of Amazon SQS Messaging under Injected Consumer and Downstream Failures'. We address the gap in Kyrychenko et al. (2025b) where The baseline evaluates SQS standard operations under steady load but misses real-world downstream failures such as consumer crash loops, throttling, and API timeouts. This research addresses this by injecting systematic faults into the SQS handlers.. Through simulation, significant improvements in throughput and cost efficiency are demonstrated.
