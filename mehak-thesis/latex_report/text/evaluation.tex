%% ===============================
\subsection{Experiment 1: Formal metric suite on synthetic harness}
\label{sec:evalOne}
%% ===============================

The evaluation harness emits Accuracy, Precision, Recall, Macro-F1, ROC-AUC and prediction latency for MHSA variants, the reactive threshold monitor, and classical RF/KNN/SVM monitors on the same synthetic feature matrix. This closes the \emph{metric-column} gap in the artefact relative to formal CA2 wording, but \textbf{does not} close the dataset gap: scores are not transferable to GCT without re-running on real traces.

%% ===============================
\subsection{Experiment 2: Classical monitors (scaffold)}
\label{sec:evalTwo}
%% ===============================

RF, KNN and linear SVM monitors are fit on flattened history windows as traditional baselines named in the formal proposal. On synthetic data, RF reaches the highest accuracy (0.953) while MHSA variants achieve higher macro recall ($\sim$0.72) and ROC-AUC ($\sim$0.92). These comparisons are scaffold-only. A faithful Aldomi et al.~\cite{Aldomi26} SelectKBest+GRU hybrid on GCT task/usage features remains blocked until \texttt{DATA\_GAPS.md} files are present.

%% ===============================
\subsection{Experiment 3: MHSA variants (architecture only)}
\label{sec:evalThree}
%% ===============================

MHSA-PerHead and MHSA-Fused share the artefact encoder; fusion is an optional architectural refinement. Synthetic differences between them are small relative to seed noise and are \textbf{not} framed as formal CA2 success. Thapliyal~\cite{Thapliyal26} is related architecture context only; the binding baseline family is hybrid/traditional monitors on GCT.

%% ===============================
\subsection{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
%% ===============================

No GCT Accuracy/ROC-AUC claim is made. AWS Lambda/Kinesis deploy stubs in the repo are unused and \textbf{not required} by formal CA2 (Colab/EC2 are listed only as optional training compute). Next evidence step is ingesting a minimal GCT subset per \texttt{DATA\_GAPS.md} and implementing the usage$\leftrightarrow$event join before \texttt{python scripts/train\_and\_evaluate.py --dataset gct}.
