%% ===============================
\subsection{Experiment 1: Formal metric suite on GCT}
\label{sec:evalOne}
%% ===============================

Windows join \texttt{task\_usage} to \texttt{task\_events} on $(\mathrm{job\_id},\mathrm{task\_index})$ with a 10-step history and 5-step horizon. FAIL/EVICT/KILL/LOST in the future window force L2 on all heads. Extra 2011 parts plus fail-series-first sampling raise fail-forced windows from 489 (MV subset) to 981.

%% ===============================
\subsection{Experiment 2: Classical and Aldomi hybrids}
\label{sec:evalTwo}
%% ===============================

RF/KNN/SVM use flattened 4-channel histories. Aldomi~\cite{Aldomi26} uses SelectKBest ($k{=}12$ of 13 expanded columns on this run) + GRU embeddings + RF/KNN. That is the paper's \emph{family} (SelectKBest, GRU extractor, GRU-RF best), not their 2019 Borg preprocessing.

%% ===============================
\subsection{Experiment 3: FN/FP (last seed)}
\label{sec:evalThree}
%% ===============================

Last-seed binary unhealthy CMs ($n_{\mathrm{test}}{=}2400$): MHSA-Fused TN=2144, FP=49, FN=144, TP=63; Aldomi GRU-RF TN=2185, FP=8, FN=133, TP=74. Both miss a large fraction of fail-horizon windows (FN). Aldomi GRU-RF has fewer false positives on this seed.
