\label{sec:Conclusion}
%% ==================

Formal CA2 asks whether MHSA-TDL improves cluster-health / failure prediction on Google Cluster Trace versus hybrid and traditional monitors, under Acc/Prec/Rec/F1/ROC-AUC/latency. High FN counts further caution against treating accuracy as success. That comparison --- including the negative MHSA outcome --- is the evidence that answers the RQ.
