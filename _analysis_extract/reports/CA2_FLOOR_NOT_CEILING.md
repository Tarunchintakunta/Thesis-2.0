# Research bar: CA2 is a floor, not a ceiling

**Policy (2026-09-20):** Formal CA2 proposals are the **minimum** research contract. Cohort work **may and should exceed** CA2 where it strengthens evidence (deeper $n$, more cells, stronger baselines, better packaging, extra metrics).

## Rules
1. **Satisfy CA2 objectives** with evidence-bound claims (or document an explicit, justified scope change in Design/Rationale).
2. **Exceedances are first-class** — record them under Design / Rationale / STATUS as “beyond CA2” with why (validity, power, replication, practitioner utility).
3. **Do not demote** a thesis for answering *more* than CA2 asked, as long as honesty holds (no invented numbers; Free Tier; destroy-after-round).
4. **COMPLETE** when: CA2 floor met **and** residual soft gaps are either closed or explicitly superseded by a stronger beyond-CA2 design with rationale — not when every optional CA2 footnote is unchecked.
5. Proxy artefacts that answer a **different RQ** still need remapping or quarantine; “better than CA2” ≠ “different thesis.”
