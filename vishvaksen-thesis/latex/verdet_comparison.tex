\section{Comparison to Verdet et al.\ (2025): McNemar and Holm--Bonferroni}
\label{sec:verdet-mcnemar}

Verdet et al.\ (2025) report scanner agreement mainly on disputed
Checkov/tfsec cases and do not publish labelled Terraform recall/F1.
On this labelled corpus ($N{=}240$ modules; $144$ insecure) the measured
identification rates (recall) are: scripted checklist $81.2\%$,
Checkov~$56.2\%$, tfsec~$61.8\%$, static union~$70.8\%$,
OPA~$60.4\%$.

Exact two-sided McNemar tests on discordant detections among the $144$
insecure modules yield:
Checkov vs.\ tfsec $b{=}13$, $c{=}21$, $p{=}0.229$;
static union vs.\ OPA $b{=}35$, $c{=}20$, $p{=}0.058$;
checklist vs.\ static union $b{=}34$, $c{=}19$, $p{=}0.053$;
checklist vs.\ OPA $b{=}30$, $c{=}0$, $p{=}1.86\times10^{-9}$.

Holm--Bonferroni correction across these four pre-registered pairs at
$\alpha{=}0.05$ leaves a single rejection: checklist vs.\ OPA
(adjusted $p{=}7.45\times10^{-9}$). The other three pairs are not
significant after correction (adjusted $p\in\{0.160,0.160,0.229\}$).

These contrasts concern labelled detection disagreement; they are not
Verdet adoption metrics. Checklist figures are scripted, not
the label-oracle checklist scored against ground-truth labels.
